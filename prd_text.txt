1. Executive Summary & Vision
Vision Statement
Circles is the digital twin of the cafe and the library — an anti-vanity social network that seamlessly transforms shared interests into authentic, live conversations. It brings back the lost Third Place by replacing algorithmic outrage and performative metrics with deep content consumption and serendipitous, organic human connection.
Core Paradigm
Users do not come to Circles primarily to chat. They enter a Reading Room to consume high-value content precisely tailored to specific facets of their identity. Social interaction is entirely opt-in — an organic byproduct of occupying that intellectual space — replicating the feeling of making eye contact and striking up a conversation with a stranger reading the same book at a teashop.
The Opportunity in One Line
Every existing social platform optimises for attention. Circles optimises for resonance.
Founding Context
Circles is the pilot product of CausalSyntax — a product company founded by Arun Mohanan with the mission of building human-centric digital spaces that respect attention, protect identity, and reward depth of engagement over breadth of reach. CausalSyntax is the house under which Circles and future products will be built, headquartered at causalsyntax.com.

Identity Architecture: Dual-Identity System
Base Profile (Private): Legal name, master email, date of birth — stored securely, never surfaced publicly.
Personas (Faceted): Multiple isolated identities per user. Each Persona has its own alias, avatar, interest tags, Feed Wall, and collision history.
Privacy Matrix: Users explicitly toggle which Base Profile attributes (e.g., Location: 'Kerala, India') are shared on each Persona to establish contextual trust without full disclosure.
No Context Collapse: Each Persona is hermetically sealed. Activity, conversations, and connections in one Persona are invisible to others.
4. Product Architecture & Core Mechanics
Circles is structured as six sequential interaction phases, each unlocking the next through explicit user intent.
Phase 1 — The Reading Room (Feed Wall)
The anchor experience. A curated, single-player library ensuring zero cold-start friction.
Content Mix: RSS-pulled articles, global news APIs, study reports, native webinars, and curated book recommendations — all mapped to the active Persona's interest tags.
Zero Vanity: No public likes, no comment sections, no follower counts.
Book Shelf: Curated books are surfaced inline in the Feed Wall alongside articles. Users add titles to a personal Reading List. For physical books, an affiliate purchase link routes to retail partners. For paid eBooks, the user can purchase and read entirely within the app via an embedded reader. Free eBooks open directly in-app. Circles earns affiliate commission on physical book sales and a platform margin on in-app eBook purchases.
Contextual Sponsored Cards: Domain-matched sponsored content (courses, certification programmes, workshops) appears as clearly-labelled cards in the Feed Wall — at most 1 per 10 items. Sponsored cards are never shown inside active Lobbies, Tables, or Live Circles. See Section 7 for the full Sponsored Connection mechanic.
Phase 2 — The Ambient Lobby (Passive Matchmaking)
Collision Engine: A background service continuously detects overlapping online Personas sharing interest tags and generates transient Lobbies.
Ambient Cue: The user's active Persona avatar on the Feed Wall begins to gently glow — a soft, non-intrusive signal of co-presence.
Lobby View: Switching to the Persona Dashboard reveals active Lobby cards (e.g., '6 Personas exploring Solopreneurship') including each Persona's alias, disclosed location, and tags.
Phase 3 — The Open Note & Handshake (Icebreaking)
Open Note: A short, contextual text message (max 150 characters) dropped into the Lobby, visible to all occupants.
Handshake: Any Persona can reply directly to an Open Note. The original author accepts or ignores — no unsolicited audio, ever.
Phase 4 — The Table (Audio Micro-Channels)
Spatial Isolation: Upon a successful Handshake, the two Personas' avatars snap together visually into a 'Table' within the Lobby UI.
Audio-First: An isolated WebRTC audio channel opens exclusively between them. Other Lobby occupants cannot hear the conversation.
Dynamic Expansion: While at a Table, users can still read Lobby notes and Handshake a third Persona, seamlessly merging them into the existing audio channel.
Phase 5 — The Bloom & Roots (Persistence)
Bloom Request: Any Table member requests a private space. Unanimous consent from all Table members extracts the group into a dedicated Live Circle.
Video Escalation: Inside the Live Circle, any user can request cameras on. This requires unanimous consent; without it, the room remains strictly audio.
No-Trace Rule (Default): All conversation data evaporates on exit. Groups may opt to 'Plant Roots' to save the Circle and enable future async meetups.
Phase 6 — The Stage (Broadcasting & Webinars)
Event Scheduling: Verified Personas or organisations schedule Stage events (Free or Ticketed) with tags for discoverability.
Discovery: Stage cards embed natively in relevant Personas' Feed Walls.
Mechanics: Broadcaster (A/V enabled) + Audience (listen-only) model with a Stage Sandbox for Q&A notes.
Replays: Hosts record and publish Stages; replays appear in attendee feeds.
5. Detailed User Flows & Edge Cases
Flow 1 — Onboarding & Identity Generation
Step 1: Auth: Magic Link (email) or OAuth (Google/Apple). No password required.
Step 2: Base Setup: User submits private PII: Full Name, Date of Birth, Master Email. Stored encrypted; never displayed.
Step 3: Persona Creation: User defines Alias, selects Core Interest Tags (min 2, max 10), sets Privacy Matrix toggles, and selects an avatar from a generated set.
Step 4: Entry: App drops user directly into the Reading Room for Persona A. Feed is immediately populated — zero cold-start.
Edge Cases:
User skips Persona creation → Blocked with a contextual prompt explaining why Personas are required; cannot proceed without at least one.
Magic Link expires (>15 min) → One-tap resend with a 60-second cooldown and a clear error message.
Underage user detected (DoB < 18) → Graceful rejection with explanation; account not created.
Flow 2 — The Lobby & Connection
Trigger: Avatar glows softly while user reads. Tap → Persona Dashboard flips open.
Lobby Selection: User taps a Sandbox Card (e.g., '4 active now — UX Design').
Open Note: User types (max 150 chars): 'Anyone transitioning from Figma to code?'
Handshake: User B replies. User A accepts. Avatars snap together. Microphones activate.
Edge Cases:
Lobby dissolves mid-session (all others leave) → User is gracefully returned to the Feed Wall with a soft toast notification.
User ignores Handshake for >5 min → Request auto-expires. No notification spam.
User drops a note but then switches Persona → Note is retracted from Lobby automatically.
Microphone permission denied → Prompt with OS-level settings deeplink; cannot enter audio without permission.
Flow 3 — Bloom, Video Escalation & Roots
Bloom Request: Any Table member taps 'Bloom'. All members receive a consent prompt with a 30-second window.
Rejection Handling: If any member declines, Bloom is cancelled and all parties remain at the Table. No hard feelings mechanic — decliner is anonymous.
Video Escalation: Inside Live Circle, 'Request Video' triggers a consent modal for all participants. Any single decline keeps the room audio-only.
Plant Roots: On exit, a prompt offers 'Save this Circle?' — opt-in only. Saves Persona aliases (not Base Profiles) and a title.
Edge Cases:
Network drop mid-Bloom → User is held in a reconnect state for 30 seconds before being gracefully removed. Other participants are notified with a subtle indicator.
User closes app during active Table → Session is held for 60 seconds; on return, they rejoin seamlessly.
All-hands consent timeout (30s) → Request expires, status quo maintained.
Flow 4 — Guest Drop-In via External Link
Step 1: Host taps 'Invite' → generates a magic share URL with a 24-hour TTL.
Step 2: Non-user clicks link. App/PWA opens. Prompted only for a Temporary Alias and one Interest Tag.
Step 3: Dropped directly into active Live Circle audio — bypasses full onboarding.
Step 4: On session end, prompted to create a full account. Guest session data is not retained.
Edge Cases:
Link expired → Clear error screen with option to request a new link from the host.
Circle ended before guest joins → Informative landing screen; option to join the host's Lobby instead.
Guest attempts to Bloom or Plant Roots → Blocked with a prompt to create a full account.
Flow 5 — Hosting a Stage
Scheduling: Host creates Stage: title, tags, date/time, free vs. ticketed (Stripe integration), max audience size.
Discovery: Stage card appears on matched Personas' Feed Walls up to 7 days before the event.
RSVP & Join: Users tap RSVP. At T-0, tap 'Join Stage' → enter as audience (listen-only).
Q&A: Audience drops notes into the Stage Sandbox. Host promotes notes to 'live question' status.
Recording & Replay: Host toggles recording. On end, transcript + audio is saved. Replay pushed to attendee feeds within 2 hours.
Edge Cases:
Host disconnects mid-Stage → 60-second reconnect window shown to audience; if host doesn't return, Stage ends gracefully with an automatic replay post.
Ticketed Stage — payment failure → User cannot join; shown payment retry flow.
Max audience hit → Overflow waitlist shown with live count.

System Overview
Circles is a hybrid real-time/async platform. The architecture separates concerns cleanly into four service domains: Content, Identity, Presence, and Communication.

Data Privacy & Compliance
Encryption at Rest: All PII in Base Profiles encrypted with AES-256 at the database level.
Encryption in Transit: TLS 1.3 for all API traffic; DTLS/SRTP for all WebRTC streams.
No-Trace Rule Implementation: Live Circle audio/video is never persisted server-side by default. Signalling metadata (room ID, Persona IDs) is purged within 24h of session end unless Roots are planted.
GDPR / DPDP Compliance: Right to erasure implemented at both Base Profile and individual Persona levels. Data export includes all Persona activity but never cross-Persona linkage.
Persona Isolation: Database-level row-level security ensures zero cross-Persona data leakage — not just application-layer logic.
MVP Technical Constraints & Decisions
No ML in MVP: Feed ranking is chronological + tag-match weighted. True personalisation is a Phase 2 investment.
Audio-First Priority: Video is gated behind explicit consent and is deprioritised in the MVP build to reduce LiveKit cost and complexity.
LiveKit vs. Daily.co: LiveKit is open-source and self-hostable, reducing per-minute costs significantly for a pre-revenue product.
Mobile-First, PWA Second: React Native (Expo) for iOS/Android. PWA for Guest Drop-In links only.
Scalability Gate: Collision Engine is the most computationally intensive component. Cap Lobby size at 50 concurrent Personas in MVP; shard at scale.

Feature Deep-Dive: Sponsored Connections
Sponsored Connections replace traditional interruptive advertising with a consent-gated, privacy-first mechanic. A sponsor (a course provider, certification body, professional programme) gets in front of a hyper-relevant audience. The user retains full control — no cold calls, no spam, no data leakage.
The Sponsored Connection Flow
Step 1 — Card Impression: A sponsored card appears in the Feed Wall, clearly labelled 'Sponsored' with the sponsor's Persona tag. Card contains: offering title, 2-line description, and a single CTA — 'I'm Interested'.
Step 2 — Interest Flag: User taps 'I'm Interested'. A bottom sheet appears prompting them to select their available windows (e.g., 'Weekday evenings', 'Weekend mornings'). User submits. Their Persona ID and availability preferences are passed to the sponsor — zero PII.
Step 3 — Sponsor Schedules: Sponsor sees a dashboard of anonymised Persona IDs who flagged interest, with their availability windows. Sponsor proposes a session time within the user's stated window.
Step 4 — User Confirms: User receives an in-app notification: 'A sponsor has proposed a session on [date] at [time]. Accept or Decline?' User can review the sponsor's Persona card before accepting.
Step 5 — The Session: On acceptance, a dedicated Consultation Circle (private Live Circle) opens at the scheduled time, connecting the user and the sponsor's representative. Audio-first; video requires user consent. User's contact info is never shared at any point.
Step 6 — Post-Session: User can rate the sponsor (1–5 stars, internal only). Sponsors with < 3.5 average rating are suspended from the platform. User can permanently block the sponsor's future cards.
Sponsor Safeguards & Quality Controls
Tag Matching Threshold: A sponsored card only appears on a Persona's Feed Wall if the sponsor's declared interest tags overlap ≥ 3 tags with the Persona. No untargeted broadcast.
Frequency Cap: Maximum 1 sponsored card per 10 Feed Wall items per Persona per day. Hard-coded; not a configurable limit for sponsors.
No Lobby / Table / Circle Ads: Sponsored content is strictly confined to the Feed Wall. The ambient social space is permanently ad-free.
Sponsor Verification: All sponsors must complete organisation verification (GST/CIN number for Indian entities, equivalent for international) before accessing the platform.
User Opt-Out: Any user can toggle 'No Sponsored Cards' per Persona at any time. Circles Pro users have this enabled by default.
Unit Economics (Pre-Seed Targets)
Target CAC (organic-first): < ₹500 per activated user (defined as 2+ completed Handshakes).
Target LTV (Circles Pro): ₹3,999/yr × average 2.5 year retention = ~₹10,000 LTV.
LTV:CAC Ratio Target: > 20:1 by Month 18 (achievable given near-zero paid acquisition in early cohorts).
Gross Margin Target: > 70% on subscription revenue. eBook sales, Stage fees, and Sponsored Connections carry ~15–20% blended take rate. Physical book affiliate is near-100% margin.
Anti-Patterns Explicitly Avoided
No advertising that interrupts active Lobbies, Tables, or Live Circles — ever.
No selling or sharing individual-level user data under any commercial model.
No cold-contact from sponsors: sessions only happen after explicit user Interest Flag and confirmed scheduling.
No engagement-maximisation algorithms — feed ranking is relevance-weighted, not outrage-weighted.
No dark patterns on subscription cancellation (one-tap cancel, no guilt loops).
No DRM restrictions that prevent a purchased eBook from being read offline (Circles Pro feature).

11. Appendix
A. Glossary
Base Profile: The private, legally-accurate identity layer. Never public.
Persona: A user's faceted public identity within a specific interest domain.
Privacy Matrix: The per-Persona toggle system controlling which Base Profile attributes are disclosed.
Feed Wall: The Reading Room's main content surface — the library.
Collision Engine: The background service detecting co-present Personas with matching tags.
Ambient Cue: The avatar glow indicating an active Lobby is available.
Open Note: A 150-character public message dropped into a Lobby.
Handshake: A mutual, explicit consent mechanism to open an audio channel.
Table: An isolated audio micro-channel between 2–N users within a Lobby.
Bloom: The unanimous-consent mechanism to extract a group into a private Live Circle.
Live Circle: A private, ephemeral audio (optionally video) room.
Plant Roots: The opt-in mechanism to persist a Circle for future async meetups.
The Stage: A structured broadcast event (free or ticketed) with a broadcaster/audience model.
WRC: Weekly Resonant Connections — the North Star Metric.
Book Shelf: The curated book recommendation layer of the Feed Wall; supports free eBooks, paid in-app eBooks, and physical affiliate purchases.
Reading List: A per-Persona saved list of books (capped at 20 for free users; unlimited for Circles Pro).
Sponsored Connection: A consent-gated, privacy-preserving advertising mechanic: a sponsor surfaces a card in the Feed Wall; the user raises an Interest Flag; a session is scheduled and held inside the app with zero PII transfer.
Interest Flag: The user's explicit opt-in signal of interest in a sponsor's offering, accompanied by their stated availability windows. No PII is included.
Consultation Circle: A private, scheduled Live Circle between a user and a sponsor's representative, opened only after mutual confirmation. Replaces cold-contact entirely.
